Decision Frameworks

The Tension: Concise vs. Complete

Concise

Fast, minimal friction.
"Payment failed."

Complete

Comprehensive, reduces anxiety.
"Payment failed because... do this..."

Balanced

Context-aware middle ground.

The Decision Matrix

User Context
  • Mid-Task: Concise (Don't interrupt)
  • Decision Point: Complete (Reduce anxiety)
  • Error State: Balanced (Clarity + Action)
Risk Level
  • High (Financial): Completeness Wins
  • Low (Nav): Conciseness Wins
