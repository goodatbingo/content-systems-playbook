# Examples (TIAA-Style)

## High-risk payment failure (mobile, authenticated)

- **What**: Your payment couldn't be processed
- **Why**: This card was declined by your bank
- **Impact**: Your order wasn't placed
- **Recovery**: Try a different card or contact your bank
- **Support**: If this continues, chat with support and share ID PAY-####

## Medium-risk profile change confirmation

- **What**: Your contact email was updated
- **Recovery**: Undo this change within 30 minutes from Settings
- **Support**: If you didn't make this change, contact us immediately

## Low-risk navigation hint

- **What**: Statements are under Documents
- **Recovery**: Go to Documents to download your latest statement
